About the platform

Cryptography System Overview

ENCODEX is an educational cryptography platform designed to help learners explore classical and modern encryption methods through real-time tools, mathematical explanations, and structured step-by-step examples.

Supports classical ciphers, modern encryption, hashing, key exchange, and block cipher concepts.
Turns cryptographic math into readable examples with formulas, matrices, XOR steps, and final results.
Combines live encryption and decryption with guided learning cards for each algorithm.
